# Operation Knightfall

Operation Knightfall is a large-scale Star Wars themed Minecraft mod built using NeoForge.  
The goal of this project is to provide a "mostly" lore accurate and open world experience, allowing players to choose from many progression paths (i.e. bounty hunter, sith, jedi, imperial/rebel/republic/seperatist trooper, etc.) 

This mod expands Minecraft with new gameplay systems inspired by the Star Wars universe while maintaining compatibility with Minecraft's sandbox gameplay.

---

## Project Overview

Operation Knightfall introduces a fully custom progression system and gameplay mechanics inspired by the Star Wars universe.

Players will be able to discover new weapons, develop unique fighting styles, and explore new environments tied to the Star Wars setting.

Key design goals include:

- Expanding combat through blasters and lightsabers
- Implementing RPG-style player statistics
- Introducing Force abilities and alignment systems
- Creating Star Wars themed dimensions and environments
- Developing a progression system separate from standard vanilla mechanics
- Maintaining the sandbox nature of Minecraft while introducing new mechanics

---

## Features

### Blasters
- 350+ unique blaster-like weapons
- Several different firing modes
- Each firing mode has unique stats (fire rate, recoil, accuracy, damage)
- Several different ammo types

### RPG Player System
- Separate faction reputation systems
- Various lightsaber combat stat modifiers

### Force Mechanics
- 100+ force abilities
- Alignment system
- 19 base lightsaber colors
- Unique/fluid ability progression
- Customizable lightsabers

### Equipment
- Custom armor
- Jetpacks
- Vambraces
- Shields
- Much more...

### New Dimensions (Worlds)
- Star Wars themed worlds
- Custom ores and materials
- Unique structures and environments

### Custom Game Systems
- Blaster attachments
- Nightsister magic
- Ore refinement (step above smelting)
